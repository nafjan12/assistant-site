Privacy policy

Assistant · Last updated 27 November 2026

Who this covers

Assistant is a private app with a single user: its developer, Faisal Alnafjan. It is not available to anyone else. This policy explains how the app handles the data of the accounts he connects to it.

What data the app accesses

With his permission, given through Google's own sign-in screen, the app may access:

  • Gmail: read messages and settings; create drafts; send email only after he approves each message with Face ID. Every account, including work accounts, can send only after he approves each message with Face ID.
  • Google Calendar: read events and free/busy times; create or change events only after his approval. Work calendars are read-only.
  • Google Drive: read and search files (read-only).
  • Google Chat: read spaces and messages (read-only).
  • Basic account info: the email address of the signed-in account, to confirm which account is connected.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the app's features to its user: showing what needs his attention, summarising messages and meetings, finding free time, and preparing drafts for his approval. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone for their own purposes.

To prepare summaries and drafts, relevant parts of the content are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. They are sent only to produce the result he asked for. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, that data is not used to train models.

Where the data is stored

  • On the developer's own server, reachable only over his private Tailscale network, not on the public internet.
  • Google access tokens are stored encrypted. Backups are encrypted with a separate key.
  • No data is stored on third-party servers other than as described above.

Limited Use

Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept only as long as needed for the app's features; meeting audio is deleted after 30 days by default.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is removed, the app can no longer read the account, and the stored token is deleted.
  • All stored data for an account can be deleted from the app's settings.
