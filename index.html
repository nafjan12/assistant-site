Assistant

A private personal assistant. Not a public product.

Assistant is a personal app that Faisal Alnafjan built for his own use. It runs on his own server, reached only over his private network, and works for one person: him. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and no one else can use it.

What it does

  • Reads his email, calendar, Google Drive and Google Chat to prepare a daily list of things that need his attention.
  • Drafts replies and calendar changes. Nothing is sent or changed until he approves it himself with Face ID.
  • Keeps his tasks, reminders and decisions in one place.

Google data

Assistant's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the privacy policy.
